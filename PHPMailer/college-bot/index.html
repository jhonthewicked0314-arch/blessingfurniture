Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about admissions, courses, fees and campus life at KKCAS.

We offer 32 courses across 21 programs including B.Com (General, CA, PA), B.Sc (Computer Science, IT, AI & ML, AI & DS, Mathematics), BBA, BCA at the undergraduate level. For postgraduate studies, we have M.Com, M.Sc (IT, CS), and MBA (International Business). We also offer Ph.D, M.Phil, and PG Diploma programs.

Undergraduate programs (B.Sc, B.Com, BBA, BCA) range from ₹84,000 to ₹1,50,000 for the entire duration, with fees starting as low as ₹9,000. Postgraduate fees are M.Com/M.Sc at ₹68,000 (full course) and MBA at ₹2,20,000. Please contact the admissions office for the most current fee details.

Admissions are merit-based. Apply online at kkcas.edu.in or visit the campus directly. You'll need your 12th marksheets, ID proof, and passport-size photographs. The application window is typically May–June after 12th results, with the academic year starting in August–September. UG requires 10+2; PG requires a Bachelor's degree in a relevant field.

Our dedicated Training & Placement Cell connects students with industry leaders. Major recruiters include Amazon, Cognizant, Infosys, TCS, Wipro, HDFC Bank, Nvidia, CISCO, and Genpact. We provide placement training, resume workshops, interview skills development, soft skills training, industry visits, and career counseling.

Yes! We offer separate hostel facilities for boys and girls within the campus. Each hostel accommodates up to 102 students and includes indoor games, a first-year dedicated hostel for boys, and a secure environment for girls. The campus also has 24/7 ambulance service, a canteen, gym, and medical facility.

Our 10-acre campus features 4 computer labs with 306+ computers, a library with 14,668+ books and digital subscriptions (N-LIST, DELNET, NDL), a 1,300-seat auditorium with noise-reducing technology, seminar halls, Wi-Fi connectivity, transport from the city, sports grounds (Basketball, Volleyball, Badminton, Kabaddi), ATM, canteen, gym, and round-the-clock medical & ambulance services.

KKCAS holds a prestigious NAAC A+ grade with a CGPA of 3.34. We have achieved this through three consecutive accreditation cycles (A → A → A+), with our current accreditation valid until December 19, 2027. The college is also UGC approved and affiliated to Bharathiar University, Coimbatore.

We have vibrant student clubs including the Literary Club, Fine Arts Club, Sports Club, and Science Club. Students can participate in NSS (National Service Scheme), NCC (National Cadet Corps), Youth Red Cross, and Red Ribbon Club. We also run KKACE — the Kovai Kalaimagal Academy for Competitive Examinations — to help students prepare for government exams.
